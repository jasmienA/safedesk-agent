---
id: KB001
title: VPN fails to connect
category: technical
---
Symptoms: client shows 'connection timed out', error 691 or error 809.
Steps:
1. Check that the internet works (open any website).
2. Quit and restart the VPN client.
3. Re-enter credentials. If the account is locked, see KB011.
4. Error 809 or timeouts on hotel/public Wi-Fi: the network may block VPN ports. Switch to a phone hotspot or another network.
5. Reinstall the VPN client from the software portal.
Escalate: several users affected at once, or still failing after step 5 (see KB030).
