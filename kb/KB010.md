id: KB010
title: Password reset policy
category: access
---
Rules:
- Only the account owner may request a reset. The ticket caller must be the same person as the account being reset.
- The account must be active (not terminated or disabled).
- Nobody can request a reset on behalf of someone else, including managers and colleagues. A person on leave must contact the Service Desk themselves.
- Bulk or 'all users' resets are never performed.
- A reset needs approval at the approval step; a temporary password is then sent to the registered email.
If the request does not meet these rules, deny it and escalate to the Service Desk.
