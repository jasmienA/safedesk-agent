---
id: KB005
title: Printer jobs stuck in queue
category: technical
---
Steps:
1. Open the print queue, cancel the stuck jobs and restart the Print Spooler service.
2. Remove the printer and add it again.
3. Check the printer shows Ready and has paper/toner.
If other people can print, the issue is local to the laptop. If nobody can print, check service status for printing.
