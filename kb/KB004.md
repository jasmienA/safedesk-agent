id: KB004
title: Slow laptop or freezing
category: technical
---
Steps:
1. Restart (not just close the lid) and install pending updates.
2. Check free disk space: below 10 GB free causes slowness. Empty the recycle bin and downloads.
3. Disable unneeded startup apps in Task Manager.
4. Run the built-in hardware diagnostics.
5. Devices older than 5 years: raise a hardware refresh request through your manager.
Check device info (age, disk space, last patch) before advising.
