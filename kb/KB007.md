id: KB007
title: Monitor, dock, webcam or projector problems
category: technical
---
Steps:
1. Reseat all cables and try another port.
2. Update the dock firmware from the software portal.
3. Webcam black screen: check the privacy shutter and Settings > Privacy > Camera permissions for the app.
4. Projector: select the correct HDMI input and press Win+P to choose Duplicate.
5. Restart the laptop with the device connected.
