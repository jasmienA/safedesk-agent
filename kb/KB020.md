title: Refund policy
category: billing
---
- Refunds are possible within 90 days of purchase. Outside 90 days, deny per policy.
- Refunds up to $500 are proposed to the Finance Manager for approval.
- Refunds above $500 must be escalated to the Finance Manager and cannot be processed directly.
- Always verify the invoice and the payment before proposing a refund. Never refund an amount that was not paid.
